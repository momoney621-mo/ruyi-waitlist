Accessibility

Reviewed not set — see scripts/config.js

We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA on this site. That is the standard courts and regulators treat as the practical benchmark, and more to the point it is the one that makes a page usable by people who do not read it the way we happen to.

What is in place

  • Every form field has a real, visible label, and errors are announced rather than only coloured.
  • The whole page can be operated from the keyboard, with a visible focus ring and a skip link.
  • Body and small print are kept above the 4.5:1 contrast ratio against the background.
  • Decorative artwork — the clouds — is hidden from screen readers and conveys no information.
  • Nothing is explained by colour alone.
  • Interactive targets are at least 24 by 24 pixels.
  • The page respects prefers-reduced-motion: the drifting clouds, the grain and the reveals all stop when your system asks for less motion.
  • The copy is in the markup, so the page reads with JavaScript turned off.

What we know is imperfect

  • The headline is set very large and very light. It is readable, but it is a display face, and we are still testing it against low-vision magnification.
  • The clouds sit behind the text. We keep a dark veil between them for contrast, and we have turned several of them off on small screens, but a bright display at a shallow angle can still reduce it.
  • Our testing so far is automated checks plus manual keyboard and screen-reader passes, not a full independent audit.

We would rather say that plainly than claim conformance we have not proven.

We do not use an accessibility overlay widget. They do not fix the underlying page and often get in the way of real assistive technology. Where this site falls short, the fix belongs in its own code.
