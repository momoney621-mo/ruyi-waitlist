Privacy Policy

Effective not set — see scripts/config.js

This page is a waitlist and nothing else. It collects four things you type, keeps them until the private beta opens or until you ask us to delete them, and never sells or shares them. There are no advertising cookies, no analytics, and no trackers on this site.

Who is responsible

The operator of this site is not set — see scripts/config.js operates this waitlist and decides how the information described here is used. For anything in this policy, including a request to see or delete your entry, write to no contact address set — see scripts/config.js.

What we collect, and why

What Why we ask
Name So the one email we send you is addressed to a person rather than an inbox.
Email address The only way we can tell you the beta has opened. It is also the key that identifies your entry if you ask us to delete it.
Role Optional. It tells us who is waiting, so the beta opens to the people it actually fits.
Field Optional. Same reason: Ruyi is tested against real papers, and knowing the fields represented shapes which ones.
Your consent, and its wording We store the exact sentence shown next to the form, whether you ticked the optional updates box, and when. It is a record of what you agreed to.

We do not ask for, and cannot receive, anything else. There is no account, no password, no payment detail and no document upload on this site. Our hosting provider and database provider keep ordinary server logs, which can include your IP address and browser, for their own security and abuse prevention.

Ruyi will never ask you for a university or publisher password — not on this page, and not in the product. If anything claiming to be Ruyi asks for one, it is not us.

The legal basis, if you are in the UK or the EU

  • Consent (UK/EU GDPR Article 6(1)(a)) for adding you to the waitlist and emailing you about the beta, and separately for the optional product updates. You can withdraw either at any time, and withdrawing does not affect what was lawful before you did.
  • Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)) for keeping the site available and free of abuse — the honeypot field and our providers' server logs.

Who else sees it

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We have never done so and have no arrangement to. The only parties who process it are the service providers we need in order to run a website:

  • Supabase — stores the waitlist rows.
  • Our static host — serves this page.
  • An email provider — delivers the message telling you the beta is open.
  • Google Fonts — serves the typefaces, which means your browser requests files from Google when the page loads.

Each acts on our instructions and may not use your details for their own purposes. We may also disclose information if the law requires it. These providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under the transfer terms in their own agreements.

How long we keep it

Until the private beta opens and the invitations have gone out, and in any case no longer than 24 months from the day you signed up — or until you ask us to delete it, whichever comes first. We keep the consent record for the same period, because it is the evidence that the signup was legitimate.

Cookies and local storage

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising scripts. It stores one item in your browser's local storage, under the key ruyi.waitlist, holding the name and address you submitted. Its only job is to recognise that you already joined so the form does not ask twice. It never leaves your device, and clearing your browser data removes it.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to show you your entry, correct it, or delete it, and we will do it. Write to no contact address set — see scripts/config.js and say which address to look up. We aim to reply within 30 days, and we will not charge you or make you create an account to ask.

  • UK and EU: you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, and withdrawal of consent, and you may complain to your national data protection authority.
  • California: you have the right to know, delete, and correct, and the right to opt out of sale or sharing — although there is nothing to opt out of, because we do neither. We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights.

Age

The waitlist is for people aged 16 or over. We do not knowingly collect anything from a child. If you believe a child has joined the list, write to us and we will delete the entry.

Security

Entries are transmitted over HTTPS and stored in a database with row-level security enabled. The key published in this page can add a signup and cannot read the list back. No method of storage is perfectly secure, so we keep as little as possible for as short a time as possible.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the effective date above, and if the change is significant we will say so in the email we send you.
